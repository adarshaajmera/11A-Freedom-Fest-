Sanyal’s Newsstand

A shop of paper and signal, kept in the memory of Sachindra Nath Sanyal (1893—1942) — revolutionary, prisoner of the Cellular Jail, and author of Bandi Jivan.

  1. Walk the shop. Joystick, WASD or arrow keys to move. Drag to look around, or hold Shift with ← / → to turn. Hold Shift to run — on a phone, push the joystick to the rim.
  2. Read everything. Thirty-two exhibits, +40 each — through the door behind the counter there is a composing room, a passage, the jail wing, and the labour yard beyond it.
  3. Answer what it asks. Every exhibit asks a question about what you just read. Fast answers and streaks are worth more.
  4. Watch for the officer. A policeman walks the whole map looking for you. When he stops you he asks a question about wherever you are — right and he waves you on, wrong and he takes one of your three hearts. He is slower than you: run.
  5. File your score at the ledger on the counter.
